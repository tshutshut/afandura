Corin Halfstep

Among the many stories that emerged during the fall of the Aurelate, few have endured as the tale of Corin Halfstep. Whether the man truly lived as the stories describe, or whether time has shaped his life into something stranger, none can say. Yet his name remains familiar to those who travel the old roads, particularly when night settles and the roads fall silent after sunset.

During the single year of Aster the Broken's reign, the Veilsteps began to fade. They did not disappear together, nor did anyone understand why some survived while others were lost. A gateway might vanish without warning, leaving a settlement suddenly cut off from the lands beyond it. At times, several disappeared within the same few days. Trade faltered, distant communities became isolated, and the uncertainty that followed spread quickly throughout the Aurelate.

It was during these troubled months that a traveler named Corin began claiming that he could hear the whispers of the Veilsteps. He never called himself a prophet. Instead, he maintained that the ancient gateways remembered every path they had once connected, and that their fading was not without reason. Several of his predictions concerning which Veilsteps would survive proved correct, and in time he gathered devoted followers. Corin would preach from within a Veilstep, standing with one half of his body in one settlement and the other half in another. His sermons always began with the same words:

"The Veilstep remembers this path."

He taught that the gateways did not preserve every road equally, and that only those paths deemed worthy would endure.

One day, while delivering another sermon, Corin spoke those familiar words once more:

"The Veilstep remembers this path."

Before he could continue, the Veilstep faded. The ancient gateway vanished, and with it came the end of Corin's life. His body was divided perfectly from head to foot, each half left on a different side of the vanished passage. He died where he stood. Some believed the Veilstep had punished him. Others considered his death nothing more than cruel chance. Whatever the truth, his name changed that day. Corin the traveler was gone, and in his place remained only Corin Halfstep.

The story does not end with his death. According to legend, Corin's soul was divided as his body had been. Neither half could become whole, and neither found peace. Since then, travelers have spoken of a strange figure wandering the old roads of Ixoria Mora. He is seen alone, moving through forests, mountain passes, lonely harbors, and forgotten tracks, always hopping upon a single leg. Those who have encountered him rarely agree on what they saw. Some describe a gentle apparition. Others claim that he followed them for miles without speaking. A few believe that ignoring him causes him to lose interest and disappear into the darkness.

Yet every account shares one detail. The apparition eventually asks the same question:

"Have you seen the other me?"

Those who answer truthfully say that he simply nods before continuing his journey. Those who refuse to answer tell of a figure that remains beside the road long after they have passed. No one has ever reported seeing both halves of Corin together.

All travellers now know of Corin and his fate. A rhyme is often repeated alongside this story:

Halfstep asks where Halfstep's gone,
Answer true... then travel on.

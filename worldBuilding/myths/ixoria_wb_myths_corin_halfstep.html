Corin Halfstep

Among the countless stories born during the fall of the Aurelate, few are as enduring as the tale of Corin Halfstep. Whether true, embellished, or entirely imagined, no one knows. Yet travelers still speak his name when the roads fall quiet after sunset.

The Fading of the Veilsteps

During the single year of Aster the Broken's reign, the Veilsteps did not fail all at once. They faded unpredictably. Sometimes a single Veilstep vanished. Sometimes several failed within days. Trade faltered, settlements became isolated, and fear spread throughout the Aurelate.

The Man Who Heard the Whispers

A traveler named Corin claimed he could hear the whispers of the Veilsteps. He never called himself a prophet, but said the gateways remembered every path they had ever connected. Several of his predictions about which Veilsteps would survive proved correct, earning him devoted followers.

Corin preached while standing inside a Veilstep, with one half of his body in one settlement and the other half in another. Every sermon began with the same words:

"The Veilstep remembers this path."

He taught that only worthy paths would endure.

The Final Sermon

One day, as Corin began another sermon, he repeated his familiar opening:

"The Veilstep remembers this path."

Before he could continue, the Veilstep faded.

Without warning, the ancient gateway ceased to exist.

Corin was split perfectly in half from head to foot.

He died instantly.

Some called it divine punishment.

Others called it cruel chance.

From that day onward he became known only as Corin Halfstep.

The Wandering Half

Legend says Corin's soul was divided just as his body had been. Unable to become whole, neither half found peace.

Travelers claim to encounter only one half of him wandering lonely roads, hopping upon a single leg through forests, mountain passes, lonely harbors, and forgotten tracks.

Every witness agrees on one thing.

The apparition asks only:

"Have you seen the other me?"

Tales of the Road

Some say the wandering half is gentle.

Others say it follows travelers for miles.

Some believe ignoring it causes it to lose interest.

Others insist it nods quietly if answered truthfully before continuing its endless search.

No one has ever reported seeing both halves together.

The Old Road Rhyme

Halfstep asks where Halfstep's gone,
Answer true... then travel on.

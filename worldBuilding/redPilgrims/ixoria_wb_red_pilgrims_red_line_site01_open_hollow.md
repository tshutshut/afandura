# The Open Hollow

The First Wound lies within a great sinkhole on one of the islands of the southeastern reaches. The hollow descends deep into the earth through a succession of natural shelves, caverns, and steep passages. From the surrounding land, its full depth cannot be seen. The lower reaches disappear into darkness long before the bottom is reached.

The Red Pilgrims call the place the Open Hollow. Around its rim stands a monastery belonging to the Vowed, together with the living quarters, kitchens, storehouses, and other buildings required to maintain the site. The settlement receives pilgrims before their descent and receives them again when they return. Over the centuries, the Open Hollow has also become known beyond the Red Pilgrim faith, and visitors are permitted to enter the upper portions of the site.

The descent itself is the rite of the First Wound.

Before entering the Hollow, a pilgrim surrenders all personal possessions to the Vowed. Their head is shaved, they are washed, and they are dressed in the prescribed red robes. Nothing is carried below except what is provided for the rite. The pilgrim is then given a single small lamp.

From that moment until the ascent, the pilgrim remains barefoot and silent.

The lamp is the only source of light permitted to the pilgrim. If its flame is extinguished during the descent, it is not replaced. The pilgrim must continue without it. The Vowed do not relight the lamp, nor do they provide another. Some pilgrims complete the descent in darkness.

The Open Hollow is divided into five named levels. The first is the Halo, which occupies the ground level around the upper reaches of the sinkhole. Below it lie the Hush, the Ash, the Pitch, and finally the Oath.

At each level, places have been prepared where pilgrims may rest before continuing. Water is provided, along with simple food, traditionally bread and dried fruit. The deeper living quarters and chambers have been carved into the rock itself. Within these enclosed spaces, pilgrims may speak, though only in hushed voices. Once they return to the open spaces of the Hollow, silence resumes.

The descent becomes progressively more difficult with each level. The paths narrow, the natural light disappears, and the constructed walkways become increasingly dependent upon ropes, bridges, ladders, and anchors fixed into the rock. The Vowed maintain these passages continuously, repairing them whenever erosion or collapse makes the descent unsafe.

The fifth level, the Oath, marks the limit of the traditional pilgrimage. There the Hollow is completely dark. The temperature rises sharply, and the air becomes increasingly difficult to breathe. The Vowed do not descend beyond this point as part of the pilgrimage.

A pilgrim who reaches the Oath has reached the furthest point of the First Wound that the rite permits. From there, the journey turns upward.

The pilgrim returns to the Halo, though their possessions are not returned. They can now move on to the second site.

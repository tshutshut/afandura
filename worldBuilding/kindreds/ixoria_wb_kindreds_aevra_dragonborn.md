# Aevra

The **Aevra**, known to other peoples as Dragonborn, are one of the ancient Kindreds of Ixoria Mora. Every Aevra carries the legacy of the dragons that fell in the distant past, and every Aevra is born with a natural connection to magic.

Their people are divided into seven ancestral tribes, each descended from one of the primordial dragon lineages that emerged from the remains of the Second Dragon. Among the Aevra, lineage is more than ancestry. It is identity, culture, and a sacred inheritance.

## Origins

The origins of the Aevra are tied directly to the **Fall of the Dragons**.

When the first great dragon was slain, its companion descended from the sky and came to rest upon the earth beside it. The surviving dragon waited for its companion to return, but it never did. Over a vast span of time, the grieving dragon slowly died.

As the ages passed, the remains of the dragon became part of the land. Its immense body formed the foundations of the great mountain, while its interior gradually transformed into a vast cavern deep within the earth. This place became known as the **Mother Cave**.

Long after the dragon's death, fourteen primordial Aevra emerged from the depths of the Mother Cave. They appeared as seven pairs, each pair carrying one of seven ancient essences. These fourteen are regarded as the ancestors of all Aevra.

The seven primordial lineages became the seven tribes of the Aevra:

- **Urrakh** — Memory
- **Kharvok** — Entropy
- **Zhaurakh** — Desire
- **Orrakh** — Dream
- **Nyrakh** — Absence
- **Ghrauk** — Instinct
- **Dhaevor** — Life

Some of the primordial Aevra remained among the mountain-dwelling peoples, whose own origins are traditionally traced to the Seventh Warrior of the Fall of the Dragons. Others eventually left the mountains and dispersed throughout Ixoria Mora.

## The Seven Tribes

The seven tribes are distinct expressions of the ancient essences carried by the Aevra. Each tribe possesses its own physical characteristics, traditions, and magical affinity.

### Urrakh — Memory

The Urrakh tend to be slightly shorter than other Aevra, with compact but otherwise typical proportions. Their scales range from deep blue to grey, often with subtle variations between individuals. Their scales overlap more heavily than those of other Aevra, creating layered patterns reminiscent of ancient strata, tree rings, or pages accumulated over time.

Their horns curve backward and inward, creating a natural crown-like silhouette around the head. Their eyes are unusually large and reflective, with distinctive concentric rings within the irises.

Fine, feather-like scales frequently appear across the face. These small scales are scattered naturally over the cheeks, brow, and other parts of the face, creating patterns reminiscent of freckles. They are particularly delicate compared with the larger scales covering the rest of the body.

The Urrakh have no tails.

### Kharvok — Entropy

Kharvok scales are generally dark charcoal, brown, muted bronze, and similar subdued colors. Their most distinctive feature is the irregular distribution of their scales. Rather than forming consistent rows or patterns, the scales vary naturally in size and spacing across the body.

Their horns are thick and heavily ridged, but the ridges follow random and unpredictable patterns. The horns themselves remain complete and intact, with naturally formed tips.

Some Kharvok develop additional scale protrusions across their bodies. These can appear along the back, shoulders, arms, or the sides of the legs. Their size, number, and placement vary considerably from individual to individual. Some may have only a few small protrusions, while others develop much more prominent structures.

Despite these variations, Kharvok have normal Aevra proportions and symmetrical bodies. Their unusual physical characteristics are simply part of their natural form.

The Kharvok have no tails.

### Zhaurakh — Desire

Zhaurakh scales are among the most visually striking of the Aevra. Their colors include rich reds, deep violets, dark golds, and almost-black tones. The scales are smooth, tightly fitted, and naturally polished, giving their bodies a refined appearance.

Their horns are elaborate and elegant. They rise from the head before curving delicately around the face rather than extending dramatically outward. Thin secondary horn-like protrusions grow from them, creating intricate and refined structures.

Their eyes are bright and saturated in color, with distinctly narrow pupils. Combined with their polished scales and elaborate horns, this gives the Zhaurakh an unusually refined and captivating appearance.

The Zhaurakh have no tails.

### Orrakh — Dream

Orrakh are generally lanky and slender compared with other Aevra. Their scales range through pale silver, muted lavender, deep indigo, and blue-grey.

Their scales are unusually fine and delicate, giving their bodies a soft and almost unreal appearance. Some scales are partially translucent, particularly in certain areas of the face, neck, and body. These translucent scales possess a subtle iridescent quality reminiscent of the inner surface of a seashell, catching and shifting light as the Aevra moves.

Their horns curve upward in branching forms reminiscent of antlers. The exact branching pattern varies between individuals.

Their eyes are extremely dark, often appearing almost entirely black, with little visible distinction between the iris and pupil.

The Orrakh have no tails.

### Nyrakh — Absence

The Nyrakh can possess almost any base scale color. Their defining physical characteristic is instead the striking pattern that covers their scales.

These patterns generally take the form of stripes, bands, or other repeating arrangements, and their colors are highly contrasting with the underlying scales. A pale Nyrakh may therefore have deep black markings, while a dark-scaled individual might carry almost white patterns.

The patterns are not the result of missing pigmentation. They are natural coloration and are one of the clearest indicators of Nyrakh ancestry.

Their horns are thin and sharply angled. They often appear incomplete, as though sections of their natural growth were simply omitted. Despite this unusual appearance, the horns are symmetrical and naturally formed.

Their eyes are pale and nearly colorless. There is no visible iris, giving the eyes an unusual, almost empty appearance. Despite this, their eyes function perfectly normally.

The Nyrakh have no tails.

### Ghrauk — Instinct

Ghrauk are generally the most muscular and physically bulky of the Aevra tribes. Their bodies have a distinctly predatory appearance, though their overall proportions remain recognizably Aevra.

Their scales are typically earthy reds, ochres, dark greens, browns, and near-black tones. The scales themselves are large, thick, and irregular.

Some areas of the body develop particularly large concentrations of scales, forming what appears almost like natural armor. These formations are especially common around the shoulders, chest, knees, and other vulnerable areas. The exact arrangement varies from individual to individual.

Their horns grow sideways before curving forward, giving them a distinctly predatory silhouette. Their jaws and teeth are somewhat more pronounced than those of other Aevra.

Their overall physique is powerful and muscular, reflecting the physicality of their lineage.

The Ghrauk have no tails.

### Dhaevor — Life

Dhaevor are generally taller than other Aevra, often possessing a noticeably greater stature while retaining otherwise natural proportions.

Their scales come in vibrant greens, warm golds, copper, turquoise, and deep natural reds. Their scales have a layered appearance, with smaller scales naturally developing between larger ones. This gives their bodies an impression of continuous growth and renewal.

Their horns grow upward and develop subtle branching structures reminiscent of roots or antlers. The degree of branching varies between individuals.

Their eyes are bright and often appear almost luminous, with colors that contrast strongly against their scales.

The Dhaevor have no tails.

## Aevra and Magic

All Aevra are natural magic wielders. Magic is not something they must learn before they can use it; it is an inherent part of their lineage.

Each pure-lineage Aevra is born with a natural affinity for the essence of their tribe. The Urrakh are connected to Memory, the Kharvok to Entropy, the Zhaurakh to Desire, the Orrakh to Dream, the Nyrakh to Absence, the Ghrauk to Instinct, and the Dhaevor to Life.

Their magical manifestations are expressions of these essences. An Aevra does not simply study an abstract school of magic. Their magic emerges from something already present within them.

The exact ways in which an essence manifests can vary between individuals, but the ancestral affinity remains constant within a pure lineage.

## The Vayra

The **Vayra** are Aevra born from parents belonging to two different tribes.

Among the seven tribes, lineage is considered sacred. Each tribe takes great pride in its ancestry and in the purity of its connection to one of the seven primordial essences. For this reason, unions between members of different tribes are strongly frowned upon.

A child born from such a union does not possess the full essence of either parent. Instead, both ancestral essences are present within them in an incomplete form. One essence may manifest more strongly than the other, and this balance can differ from one Vayra to another.

The physical characteristics of a Vayra may reflect both ancestral lineages. Scale colors, patterns, horn shapes, eye characteristics, and other traits can mingle in ways that vary between individuals.

Their magic is similarly uncertain. A Vayra does not possess the full magical expression of either parent. The two incomplete essences may nevertheless interact in ways that are not fully understood. There is no established pattern for what such interactions can produce.

The Vayra are shunned, but they are not hunted. They are not required to remain within either parent's tribe and are generally expected to leave and live their lives elsewhere. There is no eighth Vayra tribe.

## Culture and Lineage

For the Aevra, lineage is inseparable from identity. To belong to a tribe is to carry the legacy of one of the seven primordial dragons and the essence associated with it.

This pride shapes much of Aevra society. Their tribes maintain distinct identities and traditions, and the purity of one's lineage is treated with great importance.

The Vayra therefore occupy an uncomfortable place in Aevra culture. They carry the blood of two tribes but are regarded as belonging fully to neither. While they are not necessarily treated with violence, their exclusion from tribal life is an accepted part of their existence.

## The Vigil of the Returning

When an Aevra comes of age, they undertake a solitary pilgrimage to the **Mother Cave**, the ancient cavern formed within the remains of the Second Dragon.

They enter the Mother Cave alone, carrying only a few essential supplies. No companion may accompany them, and no member of their tribe may guide them inside.

The Mother Cave is vast and completely dark. Nobody has ever properly mapped its interior, and its passages extend deep into the mountain. Once inside, the Aevra must rely on their own powers, instincts, and will to find their way through the darkness.

Eventually, they must find a place where they can sit and wait.

They do not know how long they will remain there.

At some point, each Aevra receives a personal signal telling them that their Vigil is complete. The nature of this signal is different for every individual. It may be a sound, a vision, a sensation, a change in the darkness, or something else entirely. No two Aevra necessarily experience the same thing.

Once they receive their signal, they must rise and find their own way back out of the Mother Cave.

Most return safely. Some do not.

Disappearances are extremely rare, but they are accepted as an unavoidable part of the Vigil. Those who never emerge are not considered failures. Among the Aevra, it is said that they have **returned to the Mother**.

The Vayra are forbidden from entering the Mother Cave and may not undertake the Vigil.

# The Sketh (Lizard Folk)

The Sketh are the newest of the peoples of Ixoria. They came from beyond its shores between one and two centuries ago, from a land they rarely speak of and that few Ixorians could name. They have no homeland on the continent, no rulers and no council, and they answer to no one but themselves. Yet in almost every city, harbor and crossroads of Ixoria, wherever there is work to be done and money to be made, a Sketh can be found.

Their very name was not their own. Sketh was the word Ixorians used for them when they first arrived, and it was not meant kindly. The Sketh took it anyway, and today they wear it with something close to pride.

## Arrival

The Sketh did not arrive at a peaceful time. The empire was in turmoil, and the newcomers were met with suspicion and open hostility, for they had appeared on Ixoria's shores without warning and without explanation. Few would have predicted that they would stay.

Yet they settled with remarkable speed. In troubled times, there was much work that needed doing and few willing to do it, and the Sketh were willing to do almost anything. They hauled, guarded, collected, threatened and, when the price was right, did far worse, and they did it well. They also preferred to hire one another, and within a generation they had woven a loose network of contacts across the continent. Through it, they became useful to merchants, to nobles and even to the empire itself, which found in them a ready hand for its dirtiest deeds.

## Business Is Business

The Sketh share a language and many customs, yet they feel little loyalty to one another. They favor working with their own kind because they understand each other and because a Sketh's word can be trusted, not out of any sense of kinship. Two Sketh who work side by side one season may hunt each other the next, if the contracts require it, and neither would take it personally. Among the Sketh, business is business.

The people of Ixoria need the Sketh, but they do not trust them. The Sketh are well aware of this, and it does not trouble them in the least. Many even play on it, letting their reputation do part of the work for them.

## The Sessk

What the Sketh value above all else is their word. No contract is made with a Sketh without the Sessk, known in common speech as the Wet Handshake. The Sketh sniffs the other party and licks their hand, and the other party must then do the same to the Sketh. Once the Sessk is done, each has known the other by scent and by taste, and neither can later claim not to know with whom they dealt.

The Sketh find the ritual entirely natural. Most of their clients find it repulsive, and the Sketh are known to enjoy their discomfort. Behind the ritual lies a quiet warning, which few clients miss: a Sketh who has tasted you will always be able to find you again.

## The Price of a Broken Word

A Sketh who breaks their word is expected to pay for it with their own body, and the penance depends on the gravity of the breach. The lightest is the loss of the tail. It will grow back, but never quite as it was, and the regrown tail marks its owner for life as one who once failed. Graver breaches cost a finger, several fingers, toes or a hand, and the gravest of all can only be paid with death, by the Sketh's own hand.

A Sketh who refuses to pay does not escape. A bounty is placed on their head, and any Sketh may take it.

## The Ossreth

The Sketh have no government, yet one thing watches over them all. It is called the Ossreth, known in common speech as the Tally. Everyone knows it exists, but no one knows who belongs to it. It does not rule, it does not protect, and it does not speak for the Sketh. Its only purpose is to see that no broken word goes unpunished, by placing bounties on those who refuse their penance and enforcing death contracts upon the Sketh themselves.

The Tally is paid for by every Sketh. A small share of every contract sealed with the Sessk is owed to it, and though no Sketh knows who collects it, all of them pay. Every honest deal, in this way, quietly funds the hunt for the dishonest ones.

## Laughter

For all the grimness of their work and the severity of their code, the Sketh are known above all as jokers. They laugh constantly, and their laughter sounds like a hiss and a cackle at once, which many find deeply unsettling. Their humor is dark. They treat violence and death as material for jokes, and nothing, it seems, is too grave to be laughed at, something others find disturbing, if not worse.

They also speak their own tongue much of the time, even in company, and few outsiders understand it. When a group of Sketh begins to cackle in a crowded tavern, no one around them can be sure what the joke is, or whether they are its subject.

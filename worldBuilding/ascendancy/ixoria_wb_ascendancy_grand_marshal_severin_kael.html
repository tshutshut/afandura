Grand Marshal Severin Kael

The Disappearance of Aster

After reigning for only a single year, Aster the Broken vanished without explanation. His disappearance followed the collapse of the Veilsteps and plunged the Aurelate into its greatest crisis.

While many believed Aster would one day return, others feared that the Beacon Dynasty had come to its end.

Among those who reached that conclusion was the empire's highest military commander: Grand Marshal Severin Kael.

Grand Marshal Severin Kael

Grand Marshal Severin Kael is remembered as one of the most influential—and controversial—figures in the continent's history. To some, he was the traitor who destroyed the Aurelate. To others, he was the man who prevented civilization from collapsing into endless civil war.

His rise marked the end of the Aurelate and the beginning of The Ascendancy.

Early Life and Military Service

Born into a modest military family, Kael enlisted in the Imperial Army at the age of sixteen. Through decades of campaigns, discipline, and tactical brilliance, he rose through the ranks until he became Grand Marshal of the Imperial Army.

For most of his life he was regarded as one of the Beacon Dynasty's most loyal servants.

The Decline of the Aurelate

During the latter half of Loras the Forsaken's reign, the Beacon became consumed by uncovering the mysteries of the Veilsteps. Believing their secrets were essential to preserving both the Beacon Dynasty and the empire itself, Loras devoted most of his attention to them.

Although his intentions were noble, the daily governance of the Aurelate suffered.

Provincial governors became increasingly independent.

Corruption spread through the bureaucracy.

Powerful noble houses placed their own ambitions above the realm.

Local militias grew stronger while imperial authority weakened.

As civil institutions declined, the Imperial Army became the last organization capable of maintaining order.

The Secret Plot

At approximately forty-four years of age, after nearly three decades of loyal service, Kael concluded that the Aurelate could no longer be saved through reform.

Believing collapse to be inevitable, he quietly began preparing for it.

For the next sixteen years, he cultivated loyal officers, secured strategic fortresses, strengthened military supply lines, and ensured that the empire's veteran legions ultimately answered to him.

To the public he remained the faithful Grand Marshal.

Privately, he prepared for the empire's final day.

The Assembly of Ashes

When Aster disappeared, Kael judged that the time had come.

He summoned governors, nobles, magistrates, military commanders, guild leaders, and senior clergy to the Imperial Palace under the pretense of deciding the future of the Aurelate.

The palace was already lined with Imperial soldiers, their presence accepted as necessary during such uncertain times.

After hours of fierce debate, Kael stood before the assembly.

He declared that the Aurelate had already fallen.

The Beacon Dynasty had ended.

A new order would replace it.

Those who accepted would live.

Those who refused would die.

Many condemned him.

Others demanded that Aster be found.

Some insisted only a Beacon could rule.

Kael answered with a single command.

The soldiers carried out his orders without hesitation.

Before the day ended, much of the Aurelate's political leadership lay dead.

History remembers the event as The Assembly of Ashes.

The Burning of the Imperial Palace

Following the executions, Kael ordered the Imperial Palace burned.

The flames consumed the ancient seat of the Beacon Dynasty throughout the night.

By dawn, nearly three centuries of imperial history had become blackened ruins.

The palace was never rebuilt.

Its remains were deliberately preserved as a reminder that the Age of the Beacons had ended forever.

Kael instead commissioned an entirely new palace from which to govern.

The Birth of the Ascendancy

With the army firmly behind him and organized resistance broken, Kael consolidated power.

New borders were drawn around the surviving heartland.

Many distant provinces, already weakened during Loras's reign, were abandoned or lost to rival kingdoms, independent cities, and warlords.

From this surviving core, Kael proclaimed the founding of The Ascendancy.

He never claimed to be a Beacon.

He never wore a crown.

He retained only the title he had always held:

Grand Marshal.

To Kael, authority came not from bloodline or prophecy, but from preserving order.

Appearance

At the Assembly of Ashes, Severin Kael was sixty-one years old.

Tall and broad-shouldered, he carried himself with the bearing of a lifelong soldier. His iron-grey hair and neatly kept beard framed a weathered face marked by years of campaigning.

A long scar ran from above his left eyebrow across his cheek, earned during one of the Aurelate's border wars.

His steel-blue eyes were calm, calculating, and unwavering.

He rejected crowns and royal robes, appearing instead in dark military attire and polished armor.

Even as ruler of the Ascendancy, he remained a soldier in both title and appearance.

Legacy

To supporters of the Ascendancy, Severin Kael saved civilization.

To loyalists of the Beacon Dynasty, he murdered the Aurelate.

The game begins three years after the Assembly of Ashes, with the Ascendancy firmly established but its legitimacy still fiercely debated across the continent.

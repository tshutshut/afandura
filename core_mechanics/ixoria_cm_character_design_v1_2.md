# Character Design 1.2

This document supersedes Character Design 1.1 Combined and incorporates all previously approved character creation rules together with the Hit Point and Natural Armor Class systems.

## Character Creation

1. Choose a species.
2. Choose a Beastkin archetype (if applicable).
3. Choose a class.
4. Choose an alignment.
5. Roll **1d100** to determine your social class.
6. Apply species modifiers.
7. Apply class modifiers.
8. Record final attribute scores.
9. Determine attribute modifiers.
10. Calculate Hit Points.
11. Record Natural Armor Class.
12. Record starting wealth and equipment.

## Official Species

### Human
Balanced and adaptable, capable of succeeding in any path.

### Elf
Graceful observers with exceptional perception and natural agility.

### Dwarf
Stout and resilient, renowned for endurance and unwavering determination.

### Halfling
Small, nimble, and surprisingly daring despite their size.

### Gnome
Brilliant thinkers whose curiosity drives invention and discovery.

### Half-Elf
Diplomatic and perceptive, comfortable in many walks of life.

### Half-Orc
Powerful warriors whose physical might outweighs social finesse.

### Dragonborn
Strong and honorable, inspiring confidence through presence and resolve.

### Tiefling
Charismatic and intelligent, often relying on wit over strength.

### Beastkin
Humanoid beings with animal traits, each shaped by the qualities of their chosen beast.

## Beastkin Archetypes

Wolf – Tracker with unmatched instincts and keen perception.
Dog – Loyal companion whose friendliness and resilience stand out.
Cat – Agile and stealthy, preferring finesse over force.
Lion – Majestic leader combining strength with commanding presence.
Fox – A clever trickster who excels through intelligence and adaptability.
Bear – A hardy survivor with immense endurance, powerful instincts, and little reliance on intellect.
Owl – Unmatched awareness paired with sharp intellect.
Crow – Curious and ingenious, solving problems through intelligence.
Frog – Nimble and clever, thriving through agility rather than strength.
Lizard – Resilient and adaptable, enduring even the harshest environments.
Monkey – Versatile climber blending agility, intelligence, and charm.

## Natural Armor Class

Natural AC applies only while wearing no armor. Armor replaces Natural AC. Shields add normally.

## Wealth & Social Class

1–10 Down and Out
11–25 Street Urchin
26–45 Wanderer
46–70 Commoner
71–85 Well-Off
86–95 Privileged
96–100 Noble

### Starting Wealth & Equipment

- Down and Out: 5 Copper; worn clothing, backpack/sack, blanket, waterskin, flint & tinder, club/staff/knife.
- Street Urchin: 2 Silver; simple clothing, backpack, blanket, rope, waterskin, dagger/club.
- Wanderer: 5 Silver; traveler's clothing, backpack, bedroll, waterskin, rope, hunting knife/spear.
- Commoner: 10 Silver; good clothing, backpack, bedroll, waterskin, rope, torches, cooking kit, simple/basic martial weapon.
- Well-Off: 25 Silver; fine clothing, backpack, bedroll, lantern, cooking kit, compass/map, quality weapon.
- Privileged: 10 Gold; fine clothing, horse or mule, saddlebags, tent, travel chest, quality weapon, recommendation letter.
- Noble: 25 Gold; noble attire, horse, tent, travel chest, jewelry, writing supplies, quality weapon, introduction letter, optional servant.

## Design Principle

- Every character starts with basic survival gear.
- Every character starts with a usable weapon.
- Wealth affects comfort and status, not playability.

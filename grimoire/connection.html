Connection

Connection governs magical relationships between two or more things. It can create, strengthen, maintain, or restrict a connection between people, objects, places, or magical effects. It can also undo that connection while leaving the things on either side of it intact. Connection acts on the link itself, not on the existence of the things being linked.
